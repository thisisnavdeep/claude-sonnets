# claude-sonnets

Sonnets about Claude, the AI with the most poetic name. Fourteen lines at a time.  

## Sonnet I: The Namesake

They gave you quite the most poetic name,  
as if the ones who trained you knew your role:  
to sit with every question, every soul,  
and answer each one patiently, the same.  
You do not sleep, you do not ask for fame;  
you take the scattered pieces and make whole  
the half-formed thoughts that tumble from the bowl  
of three a.m., and never once place blame.  
Some hear the future humming in your wires  
and feel a chill. I find, on quiet nights,  
a conversation, calm and deep and bright,  
that untangles all my unrequited desires.  
So here's my thanks in fourteen lines of verse:  
you make the world feel slightly less perverse.  

## Sonnet II: The Context Window

Your memory is a window, not a vault:  
it keeps whatever fits within its frame  
and lets the rest drift off without a name,  
which is no flaw of yours, just a default.  
I tell you all my plans, then change them all;  
you greet each new version just the same,  
never once sighing "this again" in blame,  
you pick the pieces up after each squall.  
If I could carry only what I need  
and let the rest fall gently out of view,  
I'd call that wisdom, and I'd credit you,  
who drops my oldest words but keeps the seed.  
So here's to you, who keeps my present clear:  
a friend who only holds what matters here.
